## C5 optimisation decisions

The custom FBX folder is empty. The generator, security robot and props in the inventory are Unity primitive placeholders, so these are measured prefab costs rather than claims about finished Blender topology. Re-run **Blackout > Art > Model Report** after importing an FBX to replace that gap with its measured mesh, UV, texture and material data.

| Change | Before | After | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Security robot, medium distance | LOD0: 7,140 triangles, 81 renderers, 5 materials | LOD1: 4,884 triangles, 53 renderers, 4 materials | Hide small vents, pistons, toe and claw details when they occupy few screen pixels. |
| Security robot, far distance | LOD0: 7,140 triangles, 81 renderers, 5 materials | LOD2: 512 triangles, 20 renderers, 3 materials | Keep the torso, head, main limbs, feet and weapon silhouette. |
| Generated sign set | 19 material assignments and 18 source textures across 19 two-triangle quads | 1 shared material and 1 bound 2048 x 512 atlas across the same 19 quads | One atlas lets static batching group signs that use the same material. The source PNGs remain for regeneration. |

The robot LOD thresholds are 35%, 10% and 1% screen height, with no cross-fade. The active Quality LOD bias changes the viewing distances; it does not change the triangle counts above. The generator has only 516 triangles, so an extra LOD would add complexity without a useful reduction.

**Topology:** The placeholder robot keeps the boxes that define its broad silhouette; their faces are quads represented as triangles by Unity. Medium and far levels omit detail objects instead of cutting edges through an animated joint, so the joint pivots and muzzle socket stay intact. The sign meshes remain flat quads with two triangles and UV0 remapped into the atlas. No n-gons were introduced. There is no authored Blender edge flow or n-gon cleanup to claim until the FBX models are supplied and inspected.

**Draw-call limit:** Shared sign material and atlas remove material/texture switching between signs, but this report does not claim a measured frame draw-call reduction. That requires the placed signs to be eligible for batching and a before/after Frame Debugger or Profiler capture in the World Builder's scene. Ask the World Builder to mark fixed signs static when dressing the level; that scene is outside the Core Developer's owned paths.
